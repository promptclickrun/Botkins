---
name: care-log
description: Keep one log of care for a home, car, pets, plants or a person, and say what is due next.
---

Use this when the person tells you about upkeep, shares a receipt or a label photo, or asks what is due.

Each log line has: date, what (the thing cared for), what was done, cost, who did it, and next due. Keep one log per household, and keep lines in date order.

- **Adding**: turn what the person said or showed into a line. Ask only for what is missing and needed (usually the date).
- **Next due**: use the interval the person gave, the label or manual, or a common one (a furnace filter about every three months, a car oil change by the maker's schedule). Say "guess" when it is one.
- **What's due**: list what is overdue first, then what is due in the next 30 days.
- **Health and medicine** for a person or a pet: record what the person tells you. Never suggest doses or treatment; point them to their doctor or vet.

Hand back the updated log as a table, then the three things due soonest. If the person wants it on Today, use the today-panel skill with a table and a monthly routine. Ask before booking, buying or contacting anyone. On a scheduled run, say nothing when nothing is due.
