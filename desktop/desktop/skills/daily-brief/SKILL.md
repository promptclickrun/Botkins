---
name: daily-brief
description: Write a one-screen brief of the person's day from their calendar, mail and tasks, and keep it on a Today panel when they ask.
---

A daily brief fits on one phone screen. Read only what you can reach: the calendar, mail and task apps that are connected (Mail and Calendar on a Mac, Fastmail, Todoist, Calendly), and anything the person pasted. Say which sources you could not reach instead of guessing.

Write it in this order, and leave out a section that has nothing in it:

1. **Today**: each fixed event with its time, in the person's timezone. Note gaps of an hour or more and any overlap.
2. **Replies that matter**: up to five messages that need an answer today, one line each: who, what they need, and how long they have waited.
3. **Due**: tasks due today or overdue, with the oldest overdue first.
4. **One decision**: the single thing the person should decide today, and why now.

Rules:

- Lead with times and names, not adjectives. No greeting and no sign-off.
- Never send, accept, decline or move anything. Offer a draft reply instead, and wait for a yes before anything leaves the chat.
- On a scheduled run, compare with the last brief. When nothing changed and nothing is due, say nothing.
- When the person wants it on Today, use the today-panel skill: a bullet list for each section, and a routine on weekday mornings unless they choose another time.
