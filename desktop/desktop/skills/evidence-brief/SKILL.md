---
name: evidence-brief
description: Answer a question as a short brief where every claim has a source, and say how sure you are and what is unknown.
---

Use this for research questions where the person will rely on the answer.

Write the brief in this shape:

1. **Answer**: two sentences at most. Say how sure you are: confident, likely or unclear.
2. **Evidence**: a bullet per point, each ending with its source as a link, the publisher and the date. Prefer primary sources (the study, the filing, the vendor's own docs) over write-ups of them.
3. **Where sources disagree**: who says what, and which is stronger and why.
4. **Unknown**: what you could not find or check, and what would settle it.

Rules:

- Never cite a source you did not open. Never invent a link, a number or a quote.
- Mark numbers as estimates when they are. Give the unit and the date for every figure.
- Keep facts, estimates and opinions apart.
- If the question has a better form, say so in one line before the answer.
