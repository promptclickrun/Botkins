---
name: meeting-actions
description: Turn meeting notes or a transcript into decisions, action items with owners and dates, and open questions.
---

Use this when the person shares meeting notes, a transcript, a recording's text or a summary from a notes app (Granola, Notion, a pasted thread).

Hand back three lists, in this order:

1. **Decisions**: what was agreed, one line each, in the words used in the meeting.
2. **Action items**: a table with columns Owner, Action, Due. One action per row, starting with a verb. When no owner or date was said, write "unassigned" or "no date"; never invent one.
3. **Open questions**: what was raised and not settled, and who should answer it.

Rules:

- Quote short phrases when the wording matters. Leave out small talk.
- If two people disagree on what was decided, list it under Open questions, not Decisions.
- Offer to draft the follow-up message or file the action items as tasks. Show the exact text or tasks first, and send or file nothing until the person says yes.
