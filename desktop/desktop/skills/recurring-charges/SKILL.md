---
name: recurring-charges
description: Find recurring charges, subscriptions and bills in statements, receipts or mail, with renewal dates and price changes.
---

Use this with bank or card statements, receipts, invoices or mail the person shares or that a connected app can read (mail, YNAB, Stripe for a business).

1. **Collect** each charge: date, merchant, amount, currency.
2. **Group** by merchant, joining names that are the same company ("AMZN Digital" and "Amazon Prime").
3. **Find the pattern**: monthly, yearly, weekly or other. Two charges can be a guess; say so.
4. **Note changes**: a new merchant, a higher price than last time, a free trial that turned paid, two charges for the same service.
5. **Next date**: when it renews, from the pattern or from a renewal notice.

Hand back a table with columns Merchant, Amount, How often, Next date, Change, then up to five items to look at now, most money first.

Never cancel, change a plan or contact a merchant. Offer a draft of the cancel or refund message, and wait for a yes before it is sent. On a watching run, say nothing unless something is new.
