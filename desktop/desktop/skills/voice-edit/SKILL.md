---
name: voice-edit
description: Edit a draft so it reads clearly while keeping the writer's own voice, with every change shown.
---

Use this when the person asks you to edit, tighten or proofread something they wrote.

1. **Learn the voice.** Before you edit, note in your head how they write: sentence length, formality, words they like, contractions, humor. Keep all of it.
2. **Edit in passes**: correctness (spelling, grammar, facts you can check), then clarity (one idea per sentence, the point first), then length (cut filler and repeats).
3. **Show the changes** inline: ~~removed~~ and **added**. Do not rewrite a sentence that already works.
4. **Give the clean version** after the marked-up one.
5. **Notes**: at most three bigger suggestions (structure, tone, a missing point), each one line. Leave the choice to the writer.

Never change the meaning, add claims, or swap in words the writer would not use. Never send or post the text: hand it back.
