Lock Screen, Control Center and the Action button

Every tap is a deep link. botkins://bot/<id> opens the Botkin's last chat or a new one, botkins://chat/<id> a chat. A small widget has one tap target, plus buttons that open the app.
Picking a Botkin. The widget asks for a Botkin from the computer in use. The app keeps the list, with names, looks and states, where the widget can read it.
Live without updates. The timers count by themselves. The app reloads the widgets when a Botkin starts, finishes or needs you, and a silent push wakes it for that when it's closed.
Controls show a symbol, not a picture. Each of the 20 characters needs its outline as a custom symbol. Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button take only symbols.

Ended without a reply says how ("Timed out", "Interrupted", "Stopped") and offers Try again. Previews off: no chat title, steps or thinking, only the count.

"As it happens", within Apple's limits. A card can't take every token. The phone or the computer sends the newest step and sentence a few seconds apart (one update can hold about 4 KB), and the time counts on the phone.
What the card carries. Each update has the phase, the step count, the last three steps (tool, label, state), one line of thinking, the provider and the model. Labels are cut to 60 characters and thinking to 120.
Owner's call: is thinking on the Lock Screen private enough? Today a card's state reaches Apple as plain text, so only the step count and a short label go. Steps and thinking are the bot's content. Proposal: seal them with the key the notification extension already holds, and open them when the card is drawn. This has to be tried on a real phone first.
Settings › Notifications › Live Activities gets "Show steps" and "Show thinking". With previews off the card shows only the count, as now.

The card is keyed by Botkin. BotkinActivityAttributes moves from one chat to one bot. Its state holds up to three chats (title, phase, step, start, end, provider, model) and a count of any more ("+1 more").
One chat or several. With one chat running, the card shows its steps and thinking (section 3). With two or more, one row each, and the header says how many are working.
Which Botkin the island shows. Each card gets a relevance score: needs you, then the newest step, then done. A third Botkin shows only on the Lock Screen, since iOS shows at most two in the island.
Ending. A chat that finishes stays as a row for 30 minutes (LINGER_MS). The card ends when every chat on it has finished and lingered. A new turn in any of that Botkin's chats takes the card over.

In the Dynamic Island

In the notification

Card and island buttons are App Intents that run in the app without opening it, and answer over the link as the request panel does. A risky Allow asks for Face ID before it runs.
Where words go. A card or a widget can't hold a text field. A typed answer goes through the notification's reply field, or opens the chat with the keyboard up.
What gets buttons. Approvals, and questions with up to three short choices. A long question, more choices, a form or a secure input shows "Open to answer", so nothing a person must read gets cut.
Owner's call: a choice sends at once. In the app a choice is picked and then sent with "Send answer". On the Lock Screen that would be two taps, so this mockup sends on the first tap and confirms on the card.
Owner's call: a fourth signed piece. Choice buttons on a notification need a notification content extension, which brings its own bundle id and profile, and an entry in ExportOptions.plist. Without it, notifications offer only the fixed approval answers and Reply…
Answered elsewhere. If the person answers on the computer or in the app, the card and the notification update to "Answered on your computer" and drop their buttons.

What it takes

The pieces, in the order they'd be built.

1. Botkin widgets. New widget kinds in targets/widgets, which already holds the Botkin pictures. The app writes a small snapshot (Botkins, looks, states, chats) to a shared App Group and reloads the widgets. The computer: a silent push when a Botkin starts, finishes or needs you, so a closed app can refresh them (iOS rations these).
2. Usage widgets. The same snapshot carries the last provider-usage-v1 answer. The computer: none.
3. Controls. One custom symbol per character (20), drawn from the kit's outlines. A control per Botkin, plus Talk and New chat.
4. Card per Botkin. Change BotkinActivityAttributes (both copies), activityState in link-host/push.mjs and src/state/activity.ts together, with tests/activity.test.ts. A phone on an older build must still show something sensible.
5. Steps and thinking. The computer sends the last three steps and one line of thinking, at most one update every few seconds. Sealing that text is the open question in section 3.
6. Answers. App Intents for Deny, Allow once, Allow for this chat, a choice, Stop and Try again. Notification actions and Reply… go through the same answer call.
